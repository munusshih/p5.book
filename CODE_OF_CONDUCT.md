# Code of Conduct

p5.book welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Help make this a respectful place to learn, experiment, and share creative work.

## How we work together

- Be kind and patient, especially when someone is learning a tool or asking a first question.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Respect different creative approaches and accessibility needs.
- Credit other people’s work and respect their privacy.
- Accept corrections gracefully and respect boundaries when someone asks you to stop.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, and sharing someone’s private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainer, Munus Shih, privately at **munusshih@gmail.com**. Include links or context if you can. Please do not post sensitive reports as public issues. Reports will be handled as privately as possible; information may need to be shared when necessary to address a concern.

The maintainer may ask someone to change their behavior, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently, depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. People involved should have a chance to provide relevant context when appropriate.

These expectations apply to this repository’s issues, pull requests, and other project spaces, and when someone represents p5.book publicly.
